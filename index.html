How easily could AI replace you?

Most IT professionals assume it's someone else's job on the line. The numbers say otherwise.

Microsoft's own research puts web developers and data scientists among the 40 occupations most exposed to AI. 41% of employers plan to cut roles where AI can do the work, and 92 million jobs will be displaced by 2030. 40% of the skills in a typical IT role will change in the same window.

Answer 16 questions about your role, your week, and your training. You'll get an AI Replaceability Score from 0 to 100 on screen, plus the two or three gaps that matter most.
